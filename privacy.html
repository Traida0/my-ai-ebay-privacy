Privacy Policy

My AI eBay Agent · Last updated October 23, 2026

My AI eBay Agent is a private application being developed for use with the owner's eBay seller account. It is not a service offered to other eBay users.

Current status

The application is in development and is not yet connected to a live eBay seller account. It currently does not retrieve or retain eBay account information.

Intended use of eBay information

When authorized, the application is intended to help the owner prepare and manage listings, respond to routine buyer inquiries, assist with order fulfillment, and review seller financial information. Data retrieved from eBay will be processed only as needed for these tasks, without retaining copies of eBay user data. This statement will be updated if the application's data practices change.

Authorization and security

The application will use eBay's official authorization process. The owner will sign in directly with eBay; the application will not ask for or store the owner's eBay password. Authorization credentials, if stored to enable continued access, will be kept in protected server-side settings and will not be displayed publicly.

Third parties

The application is planned to use eBay APIs, Cloudflare infrastructure, and an AI service to perform the functions selected by the owner. It will not sell personal information. This policy will be revised before additional processing or retention practices are introduced.
